About Vehi Care

Vehi Care is a Vehicle Health Prediction System designed to help drivers and fleet managers predict maintenance needs before they become costly failures. By analyzing idle-state sensor data, we deliver accurate remaining useful life estimates in seconds.

Our predictive engine was trained on thousands of real-world vehicle diagnostics across multiple vehicle classes — Hatchback, Sedan, SUV, and Truck — ensuring accurate, class-specific predictions for every driver.

We believe preventive maintenance should be accessible to everyone. No OBD scanners, no mechanic visits — just your vehicle's idle vitals and an instant AI-powered assessment.

Features Used in Prediction Model

Our Vehicle Health Prediction System analyzes 14 key sensor features to forecast remaining useful life:

General Specs: Vehicle Type, Age, Service Months, Total Mileage

Engine Vitals: Idle RPM, Engine Temperature, Oil Pressure, Fuel Consumption

Electrical & Safety: Battery Voltage, Brake Pressure, Tire Pressure, Accident History

Sensory Analysis: Tire Condition, Idle Vibration Level

These 14 interconnected parameters trained on thousands of real-world diagnostics enable us to deliver accurate, class-specific maintenance predictions in seconds.
